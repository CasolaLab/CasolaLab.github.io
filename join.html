Join us

We are recruiting for a Ph.D. position! We welcome inquiries from people who want to study genome evolution with computational approaches. Prior experience with genomic data analysis and programming in Python or R is highly recommended for graduate and postdoctoral positions.

Graduate students

Prospective Ph.D. and M.S. students can join the lab through the Ecology and Conservation Biology, Ecology and Evolutionary Biology, or Genetics graduate programs. Applicants should hold a B.S. in biology, bioinformatics or a related field.

Postdoctoral researchers

Prospective postdocs interested in gene evolution and comparative genomics are encouraged to get in touch to discuss fellowship and funding opportunities.

Undergraduates

Undergraduates interested in genomics, bioinformatics and evolution are encouraged to get in touch.

Get in touch

Please email Dr. Casola at ccasola@tamu.edu with your CV and a short description of your research interests.
